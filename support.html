SolarDial — on-device sun almanac for any place.

Frequently asked

Does SolarDial need the internet? No. Every sun time is computed on your device using the NOAA solar equations. The App makes no network calls and works fully offline.
Do I have to share my location? No. Location is entirely optional. You can add any place by name from the built-in city list, or by typing in coordinates and a UTC offset. The "Use my location" button reads a single fix only when you tap it.
How accurate are the times? SolarDial uses the standard NOAA solar-position model, accurate to about a minute for sunrise and sunset at typical latitudes. Times near the poles (midnight sun or polar night) are reported as having no sunrise or sunset, which is correct for those days.
What are golden hour and blue hour? Golden hour is the warm-light window when the sun is low (roughly −4° to +6° above the horizon); blue hour is the cooler window just below that (roughly −6° to −4°). SolarDial lists both, morning and evening, for the place you've chosen.
How does the shadow-length tool work? On the Dial tab, set an object height and scrub the time of day. SolarDial computes the shadow length from the sun's altitude (shadow = height ÷ tan(altitude)). When the sun is at or below the horizon there is no defined shadow.
