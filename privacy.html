Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 June 2026

The short version SolarDial collects nothing. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Every place and setting stays on your device.

What this policy covers

This policy describes how the SolarDial iOS app (the "App"), published by Ilia Filiaev (the "Developer"), handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The App has no user accounts, does not require sign-in, and does not communicate with any server controlled by the Developer or any third party.

Data the App reads on your device

  • Location (optional). Only if you tap "Use my location", the App reads a single current-location fix from Apple's CoreLocation framework to compute local sun times. It is used in memory, never stored remotely, and never transmitted. The App is fully usable without it by entering a place by hand.
  • Saved places & settings. The places you add and your preferences are stored on your device using SwiftData. This data never leaves your device.

Permissions the App may request

  • Location When In Use — optional, only when you choose to add your current location. No background location is used.

Third parties

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with or sold to anyone.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other users.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your data on our servers, there is nothing to access, export, or delete on our side. You can remove all on-device data by deleting the App.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be announced in the App release notes.
