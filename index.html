SolarDial

An on-device sun almanac: sunrise, sunset, golden hour and sun position for any place you save — computed locally, with zero network.

Privacy Policy Support

What it is

Pick any place on Earth — or use your current location — and SolarDial tells you exactly when the sun rises and sets, when solar noon falls, and when the golden and blue hours begin and end. Every number is computed on your iPhone using the standard NOAA solar equations. No network, no account.

Features

A live countdown to the next sun event, a scrubbable sun-position dial with altitude and azimuth, a shadow-length calculator for any object height, saved places you can add by name or coordinates, and a daylight-trend chart showing how the day grows or shrinks week by week.

Private by design

Zero data leaves your device. No account, no cloud, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Location is optional and read only on your device to compute sun times — the app works fully from a place you type in by hand.

Everywhere you look

A Home Screen widget with today's sunrise and sunset, a Live Activity counting down to the next golden hour, and a standalone Apple Watch app for sun times on your wrist.
